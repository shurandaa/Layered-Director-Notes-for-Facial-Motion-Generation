What the videos show.
  • Our generator outputs motion parameters, not pixels: 52 ARKit blendshapes, head pose and gaze (63 values per frame, 30 fps). All faces are rendered from these parameters by LAM, an existing avatar method that we do not train. Every character is built from one AI-generated portrait; none is a real person.
  • Our model takes no audio: the mouth is generated from the word timing of the script, not synchronized to speech. The videos are silent except in the first section.
  • Where shown, the timeline below a face lists the director notes: tendencies for the whole clip, states (seconds long) and events (single moments); black blocks mark when each word is spoken.

Adding audio-driven lipsVideos 1–2

Our notes leave the 14 speech channels of the jaw and mouth to the word timing, so an audio-driven method can supply them when audio exists. Here the speech is synthesized by text-to-speech and Audio2Face-3D drives the lips from it. Left: ours alone (mouth from word timing). Middle: ours with the 14 speech channels from Audio2Face-3D. Right: Audio2Face-3D alone, whose head and gaze stay still. In these clips our non-speech mouth channels are smoothed over 0.2 s for display. These videos have sound.

Completing what is not specified (Fig. 5a)Videos 3–10

The same standard notes of a TalkVid validation clip on two characters. Most of the time nothing is specified; each method must perform those spans itself. Retrieval pastes motion that does not match the notes, and A2F+LDN keeps the head and gaze still.

Specified intensityVideos 11–18

One instruction specified at three levels, everything else identical (clip, script, checkpoint, noise); the smile uses the notes of the user study (Sec. 4.5). Ours follows the level; TL, a single-layer timeline, has no intensity levels, so it receives the same input at all three levels. For the nod, the red line marks the eye height before the nod.

Notes

  • The renderer weakens expressions, so face regions are shown with a fixed display gain, the same for every method and column in a comparison. All quantitative results in the paper are computed on the motion parameters, not on the renderings.
  • Clips were chosen from several generated samples; the paper reports results over all samples.
